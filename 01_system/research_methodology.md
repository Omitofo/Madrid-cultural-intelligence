# Research methodology

A practical, repeatable method for maintaining the entity list and collecting event information.

## A. Reviewing an existing entity

1. Open its record in `02_entities/`.
2. Find the official website and the specific events/programme page.
3. Check the relevant date range.
4. Open individual event pages for details.
5. Confirm date, time, location, price and registration conditions.
6. Record the check date and any changes.
7. Save relevant events in `03_findings/events/`.
8. Note inaccessible pages, missing details or follow-up work in `03_findings/schedule_checks/` or `05_working_notes/open_items.md`.

## B. Discovering a new entity

Look across useful categories, not just the categories already represented:

- Royal academies and learned societies
- Universities, faculties and research institutes
- Foundations and cultural centres
- Museums, libraries and archives
- Embassies and international cultural institutes
- Think tanks and public-policy organizations
- Scientific and professional associations
- Independent cultural spaces and event organizers
- Public event directories and newsletters

Evaluate whether the entity has a meaningful public programme, relevant subject matter, accessible events, or a reliable calendar worth monitoring.

Before creating a record, search the repository for alternate names and existing records.

## C. Source priority

Use sources in this order where available:

1. Official page for the specific event.
2. Official events calendar or programme page.
3. Official registration/ticketing page linked by the organizer.
4. Official newsletter or social account.
5. Reliable third-party listing, clearly identified as secondary.

A third-party listing can be a lead, but prefer official confirmation before presenting an event as confirmed.

## D. Event facts to capture

Minimum useful fields:

- Title
- Organizer
- Date and start time
- Venue and address (if published)
- Topic or category
- Short factual description
- Price and access conditions
- Registration or ticket link
- Official event URL
- Date checked

Capture end time, speakers, language, format or accessibility details when available and useful.

## E. Frequency and maintenance

There is no assumed universal checking frequency. Follow the user's requested cadence.

When doing a batch review, record:
- which entities were checked;
- the date and date range checked;
- which official pages were consulted;
- events found;
- pages that could not be checked;
- follow-up items.

Do not treat an entity as checked merely because its record was opened. Its current programme must actually have been reviewed.
