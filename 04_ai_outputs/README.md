# AI outputs

Durable, user-facing research products generated from the entity records and findings.

Examples: a weekly agenda, monthly shortlist, subject guide or comparison report.

Outputs should:
- state the period and date they were researched;
- link to event findings and original official pages;
- distinguish confirmed facts from uncertainty;
- explain selection criteria briefly;
- preserve dated reports rather than overwriting history.

Do not treat an old output as a current event source. Recheck live details before relying on it.
